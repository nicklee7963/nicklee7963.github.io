The dot product of 
$
\vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix}
v_1 \\
v_2 
\end{bmatrix}
$
and
$
\vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix}
w_1 \\
w_2 
\end{bmatrix}
$
is $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{w} = v_1w_1 + v_2w_2$

**The dot product $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{v}$ tell us the squared length $\|v\|^2$ of a vector.**

We use the words **unit vector** when the length of the vector is 1. Divide $\vec{v}$ by $\|\vec{v}\|$.

### Perpendicular Vectors
Prependicular vectors have $\vec{v} \cdot \vec{w} = 0$. Then $(\|\vec{v}\| + \|\vec{w}\|)^2 = \|\vec{v}\|^2 + \|\vec{w}\|^2$.

**COSINE FORMULA:** If $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ are nonzero vectors then $\frac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{w}}{\|\vec{v}\| \|\vec{w}\|} = cos \theta$ 

**SCHWARZ INEQUALITY:** $\|\vec{v} \cdot \vec{w}\| \leq \|\vec{v}\|\|\vec{w}\|$  
**TRIANGLE INEQAUITY:** $\|\vec{v} + \vec{w}\| \leq \|\vec{v}\| + \|\vec{w}\|$

### A Plane in 3 Dimensions
The vectors $\vec{w}$ with $\vec{w} \cdot \vec{n} = 0$ fill a 2D plane in $R^3$
